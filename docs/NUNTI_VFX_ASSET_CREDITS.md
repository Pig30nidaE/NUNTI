# Nunti transformation VFX credits

The Android assets are under `android/app/src/main/assets/nunti-vfx/`. Each theme's `manifest.json` sets the frame grid, playback rate, placement and front/back cues in the existing 768 × 768 Studio stage. The PNGs are straight-alpha exports or derivatives of the source art below. The original downloads and Effekseer editor stay in the development workspace.

| App assets | Original effect and creator | License and processing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sea/splash.png` | SEAMPROOF, Water Splash / Ocean Blue, `85_water_splash/ocean_blue/loose_512/frame_0000.png` through `frame_0047.png`. [Publisher and free sample](https://seamproof.itch.io/water-splash-vfx) | Free sample permits modification and inclusion in finished commercial apps. The source and its derivatives cannot be redistributed as standalone assets. Cropped and sampled the 60 fps sequence at 30 fps, with one atlas for both depth passes. |
| `sea/current.png`, `sea/aqua-point.png` | BrokenDesk, `A_Undine1.efkproj` and `AquaPoint.efkproj`. [MAGICAL X SPIRAL](https://effekseer.github.io/en/contributes/MAGICALxSPIRAL/index.html) | CC0. Converted the original projects to Effekseer runtime files for offline rendering with their original textures. Recovered straight alpha from matched black and white passes, cropped and packed at 30 fps. |
| `city/city-shard-left.png`, `city/city-shard-right.png` | 13rice, `spark_02.png` and `spark_03.png`. [Radial lightning effect](https://opengameart.org/content/radial-lightning-effect) | CC0. Aligned and scaled the source frames, tinted one branch lilac, and omitted a blank mid-sequence frame to avoid a flash. |
| `city/city-electric-feeder.png` | Calinou, `lightning.zip`. [Lightning animation](https://opengameart.org/content/lightning-animation) | CC0. Removed the black matte, softened the ends, and tinted the original arcs for contrast on the light Studio card. |
| `mountain/forest-wind-left.png`, `mountain/forest-wind-right.png` | NEXT-SOFT+, `Sample/01_NextSoft01/MagicHeal1.efkproj` with `Texture/Line01.png` and `Texture/Particle02.png`. [NEXT-SOFT+ effects](https://effekseer.github.io/en/contributes/NextSoft01/index.html) | CC0. Rendered offline with an orthographic camera, recovered alpha, cropped, tinted and mirrored for two wind passes at 24 fps. |
| `mountain/forest-bloom.png` | HATO, `Flowersbloom/sakura1.efkefc` with its petal model and textures. [HATO-Springhascome](https://effekseer.github.io/en/contributes/HATO01/index.html) | CC0. Rendered offline, recovered alpha, and tinted petals warm amber. The two staggered cues preserve the rendered aspect ratio. |
| `mountain/forest-leaves.png` | BrokenDesk, `A_Sylph1.efkproj` and `Texture/particle2.png`, with Kenney `PNG/Shaded/sprite_0082.png`. [MAGICAL X SPIRAL](https://effekseer.github.io/en/contributes/MAGICALxSPIRAL/index.html), [Foliage Sprites](https://kenney.nl/assets/foliage-sprites) | CC0. Adapted the authored particle effect with the shaded leaf texture. Rendered and packed transparent leaf and pollen frames at 30 fps. |

Effekseer 1.80.8 was used as an offline renderer for the CC0 project files. The Android app composites the resulting PNGs over the approved character plates with Canvas, keyed to the existing transformation timing. [Effekseer CC0 sample gallery](https://effekseer.github.io/en/contribute.html)
